Esports in Cameroon in 2026: where to compete?

By the Case Game team · 30 October 2026

In short: Cameroonian esports is growing. In 2026, Cameroon qualified for the Esports Nations Cup in the fighting game FATAL FURY, and player communities meet every month in Douala and Yaoundé. For players who want to improve, regular tournaments at gaming venues remain the most accessible way in.

Cameroon on the international stage

In July 2026, the Esports Nations Cup announced that Cameroon and Bahrain had “made history” during the qualifiers for FATAL FURY: City of the Wolves (Esports Nations Cup press release). A symbolic milestone for Cameroonian players, who had long been absent from world competitions.

Active communities in Douala and Yaoundé

According to a 2025 article on video games in Cameroon, around a hundred players meet every month in Douala and Yaoundé at “Gaming Day” events, and the country now has organised esports clubs (AllAfrica, in French). One-off tournaments have also been held in recent years, for example at the Parcours Vita in Douala.

Why start with tournaments at a venue?

  • The same equipment for everyone: same consoles, same controllers, same settings.
  • A referee on site, which avoids the disputes of online tournaments.
  • Affordable entry, often a few hundred FCFA.
  • A regular rhythm, to improve week after week and make a name for yourself.
