Our approach

The Association Case Game's tutoring is for students from New Bell and nearby neighbourhoods. Our team helps with homework, offers exercises suited to each student's level and tracks their progress week after week.

What makes it different: effort is rewarded. Every week of work earns Games Points, which students can exchange for play time at the Case Game venue. Students learn to connect effort, fun and success.

How do I enrol my child?

  1. Fill in the online enrolment form: the parent or guardian gives the student's name, class and a contact number.
  2. We call you back to choose the plan and the session days.
  3. First session at the Case Game venue, Hôtel Fatime Babylone, New Bell.

The information you send is only used to organise tutoring: see our privacy policy (in French).

A question before enrolling your child?

Message us on WhatsApp: we will help you choose the plan that suits their level.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I find tutoring in New Bell, Douala?

The Association Case Game offers tutoring from primary school to the final year of secondary school at the Case Game venue, Hôtel Fatime Babylone, New Bell (Douala), from 5,000 FCFA a month.

How much does tutoring cost?

There are three plans: Standard at 5,000 FCFA a month (2 sessions a week), Intensive at 10,000 FCFA (4 sessions and one-to-one follow-up) and Excellence at 15,000 FCFA (unlimited access, coaching and a progress report for parents).

Won't video games distract my child?

Study sessions and play time are kept separate. Play is used as a reward: Games Points are earned by working and attending regularly, which motivates students to come and make progress.

Who runs the sessions?

Sessions are run by the Association Case Game team, who follow each student and keep parents informed of their progress.
