How does it work?

  1. Get a ticket: 1 ticket = 1 hour of board games, for 100 FCFA. Tickets also come in a book of 5 for 500 FCFA, handy for parents.
  2. Take a seat at a table: games are played with 4 or 8 players. The room welcomes up to 16 players per hour.
  3. Play and win: the winner of a game gets 30 to 60 minutes of free video game time, depending on the game or event.

Mini-tournaments and events

The Case Game team runs games every day and organises board game mini-tournaments and full tournaments, as well as themed events. Board games are also part of our weekly challenges, which earn Games Points.

For parents

  • A supervised setting: games take place in the room, under the eye of our team.
  • A family logbook: we record each child's visits and the tickets used, so you know when your child came.
  • Games that help children progress: vocabulary, arithmetic, logic, patience and respect for the rules.
  • A controlled budget: the book of 5 tickets for 500 FCFA lets you plan ahead.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I play board games in Douala?

At Case Game, at Hôtel Fatime Babylone in New Bell (Douala). You can play Monopoly, Scrabble, chess and card games for 100 FCFA per hour, Tuesday to Sunday.

How much do board games cost at Case Game?

A ticket costs 100 FCFA and gives you 1 hour of board games. Tickets are also sold in books of 5 for 500 FCFA.

What can I win?

The winner of a game gets 30 to 60 minutes of free video game time at the Case Game venue, depending on the game or event. Mini-tournaments and challenges also earn Games Points.

How many people can play?

Games are played with 4 or 8 players depending on the game, and the room welcomes up to 16 players per hour. For a group, book your tables on WhatsApp at +237 6 86 84 44 07.

Can children come on their own?

Yes, from age 10. Younger children come with an adult. A family logbook keeps track of each child's visits and the tickets used.
