Case Game competitions

Case Game is a video game and esports venue at Hôtel Fatime Babylone, in New Bell (Douala). Three competition formats run all year round, for beginners and experienced players alike.

The game, date and format of each tournament (groups, knockout) are announced in advance on our Instagram and on WhatsApp. Football (EA Sports FC) is a classic of our tournaments, and other games are regularly featured.

How to sign up

  1. Sign up at the venue or on WhatsApp at +237 6 86 84 44 07, while places last.
  2. Pay the entry fee on site, in cash or by Mobile Money.
  3. Be on time: a player who arrives after the tournament has started forfeits.

Rankings based on results only

Case Game tournaments are games of skill: the ranking depends only on in-game results, with no draw or chance involved. A Case Game referee makes sure everyone plays fair, and cheating means disqualification. For the 18+ tournament, ID may be requested at sign-up and when the prize is handed over.

All the rules (cancellation, prize-giving, image rights) are set out in the tournament rules (in French).

Games Points

Weekly tournaments and the Wii League reward winners with Games Points, the venue's own currency: 500 GP gets you, for example, 2.5 hours on PS4 or Xbox. GP can also be bought in packs, from 350 FCFA for 100 GP. See the Games Points packs.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I find video game tournaments in Douala?

Case Game runs video game tournaments all year round at its venue in New Bell, at Hôtel Fatime Babylone (Douala): a tournament every week, a free Wii League and a monthly tournament for players aged 18 and over.

How much does it cost to enter a tournament?

Entry costs 200 FCFA for the weekly tournament and 1,000 FCFA for the monthly 18+ tournament. The Wii League is free.

What can I win?

The winner of the weekly tournament and the Wii League champion win 500 Games Points. The winner of the monthly 18+ tournament takes home 30,000 FCFA.

Can under-18s take part?

Yes, in the weekly tournament and the Wii League. Only the monthly tournament with a cash prize is limited to players aged 18 and over.

Can I practise before a tournament?

Yes: the venue is open Tuesday to Sunday. Half an hour on PS4 or Xbox costs 350 FCFA, and the 10,000 FCFA monthly pass gives unlimited access to the consoles.
