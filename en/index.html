FAQ

All about Case Game.

Answers to the questions we hear most often. Updated on 29 October 2026.

What is Case Game in Douala?

Case Game is a video game and esports venue at the Hôtel Fatime Babylone, in the New Bell district of Douala, Cameroon. Players use more than 10 PlayStation, Xbox and Nintendo consoles, enter weekly tournaments and play Watersoft. Case Game also runs a non-profit association for young people aged 10 to 25.

Where can I play PlayStation or Xbox in Douala?

At Case Game, in New Bell (Douala), at the Hôtel Fatime Babylone. The venue offers PS4, Xbox and Wii sessions, alone or with friends, from 30 minutes to 3 hours. It is open Tuesday to Sunday; to book or ask a question, send a WhatsApp message to +237 6 86 84 44 07.

How much does a gaming session cost at Case Game?

Thirty minutes on PS4 or Xbox costs 350 FCFA and one hour costs 600 FCFA. Wii costs 350 FCFA per hour, a Watersoft game 300 FCFA and a board game session 100 FCFA. Packs (Morning 400 FCFA, After School 500 FCFA, 3-hour Weekend 1,500 FCFA) and an unlimited monthly pass at 10,000 FCFA are also available. Prices as of 29 October 2026.

What are Case Game's opening hours?

The venue is closed on Mondays. It is open Tuesday to Friday from 12 pm to 10 pm, Saturday from 9 am to 10 pm and Sunday from 10 am to 10 pm.

How do Games Points work?

Games Points (GP) are Case Game's in-house currency. You can buy them in packs: 100 GP for 350 FCFA, 200 GP for 600 FCFA, 300 GP for 800 FCFA. You can also earn them for free in tournaments, daily challenges and community missions. 100 GP buy 30 minutes on PS4 or Xbox.

What is Watersoft?

Watersoft is a strategic team shooting game played with water-bead blasters (water-filled gel beads, Gel Blaster style). The beads burst on impact, so it hurts less than airsoft and leaves no mess. At Case Game, each game costs 300 FCFA, is open to players aged 8 and over and is always supervised by the team, with protective gear provided.

Are there video game tournaments in Douala?

Yes. Case Game runs a weekly tournament (200 FCFA entry, 500 Games Points for the winner), a free Wii League and a monthly tournament for players aged 18 and over (1,000 FCFA entry, 30,000 FCFA for the winner). Sign up at the venue or on WhatsApp.

What can children and teenagers do in Douala?

In New Bell, Case Game welcomes young people in a supervised venue: video games, board games, Watersoft and daily challenges. Its association also offers tutoring from primary school to the final year of secondary school (from 5,000 FCFA a month) and free workshops on African history, art and citizenship. Children under 10 must be accompanied by an adult.

How can I support the Case Game association?

The Case Game Association is a registered non-profit in Cameroon. It funds its free workshops and community work through donations, including its GoFundMe campaign, which is open to the diaspora. You can also become a partner or volunteer by writing to contact.casegame@gmail.com.
