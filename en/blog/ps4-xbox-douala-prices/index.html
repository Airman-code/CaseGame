Where to play PS4 or Xbox in Douala, and for how much?

By the Case Game team · 30 October 2026

In short: in Douala, people play on consoles in video game venues, paying by the half hour or by the hour. At Case Game, in New Bell, half an hour of PS4 or Xbox costs 350 FCFA and an hour costs 600 FCFA. Packs, the monthly pass and Games Points make it cheaper if you play often.

How to pay less

Pick the right time slot

The Morning pack (400 FCFA an hour) and the After School pack (500 FCFA an hour) cost less than a standard hour at 600 FCFA.

Buy Games Points in packs

Games Points are the venue's own currency: 100 GP is worth 30 minutes on a console. Bought in packs, they work out cheaper:

  • 100 GP = 350 FCFA, i.e. 30 minutes of play;
  • 200 GP = 600 FCFA, i.e. 1 hour (100 FCFA saved);
  • 300 GP = 800 FCFA, i.e. 1.5 hours (250 FCFA saved).

Earn Games Points for free

Tournaments, daily challenges and community missions earn GP without spending anything.

Switch to the monthly pass

At 10,000 FCFA a month, the pass pays for itself as soon as you play more than 16 hours in the month, about 4 hours a week.

How to choose a gaming venue in Douala

  • The state of the equipment: responsive controllers, clean screens, recent games.
  • Supervision: staff on site, especially if you send your children there.
  • Clear prices: prices on display and the same for everyone.
  • Opening hours: Case Game is open Tuesday to Friday from 12 pm to 10 pm, Saturday from 9 am to 10 pm and Sunday from 10 am to 10 pm (closed on Mondays).
  • The extras: tournaments, board games, Watersoft… to mix things up.
