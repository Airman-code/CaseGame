Activities · New Bell, Douala

Our activities

Video games, tournaments, Watersoft, board games, challenges, tutoring and community action: everything we do at Case Game, in New Bell (Douala).

Play and compete

PS4, Xbox, Wii · from 350 FCFA

Video game room

Over 10 PlayStation, Xbox and Nintendo consoles, from 30 minutes to 3 hours, alone or with friends. Pay in FCFA or in Games Points.

Find out more

Every week · from 200 FCFA

Tournaments

A tournament every week, a free Wii League and a monthly 18+ tournament with 30,000 FCFA to win. Rankings based on results only.

Find out more

Water-bead shooting · from age 8

Watersoft

The team tactical shooting game with water beads, supervised by our staff, protective gear provided. 300 FCFA per game.

Find out more

Monopoly, Scrabble, chess · 100 FCFA/h

Board games

Monopoly, Scrabble, chess and card games (Belote, Uno…) for strategy, thinking and fun together. 100 FCFA per hour, and the winner gets free video game time.

Find out more

Earn Games Points for free

Challenges and mini-challenges

4 mini-challenges a day (100 GP) and 3 big challenges a week (300 GP): street interviews, word games, blindfolded hide-and-seek, quizzes… without buying anything.

Learn and take action

Primary to final year · from 5,000 FCFA/month

Tutoring

Homework help, exercise booklets and regular follow-up, with Games Points to reward effort. Three plans.

Find out more

Free

Educational workshops

Talks and discussion workshops on African history, geography, the environment, art and citizenship.

Missions for the neighbourhood

Gamer Solidaire et Vert

Useful missions for New Bell (helping residents, clean-ups, recycling) suggested by young people and rewarded with Games Points.

Find out more

L'Arche de Mummy orphanage

Days with orphans

Days of games, workshops and sharing with the 50 children of L'Arche de Mummy orphanage, supported by our fundraising campaign.
