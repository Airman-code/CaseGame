What is Watersoft?

Watersoft is a team tactical shooting game played with water-bead blasters: small gel beads soaked in water, like those used in Gel Blasters. The beads burst on impact, which makes the game far less painful than airsoft or paintball, and they leave no stains.

At Case Game, Watersoft is played on a dedicated field in New Bell, with obstacles (pallets, tyres, barricades) to take cover behind and plan your attacks.

How does a game work?

  1. Safety briefing: a member of staff explains the rules and hands out the protective gear provided with the equipment.
  2. Teams are formed and the game mode is explained.
  3. The game: moving, taking cover, teamwork. The referee makes sure the rules are followed from start to finish.

Safety

  • Wearing the protective gear provided is compulsory for the whole game.
  • Watersoft is open to players from age 8; under-18s play with their parents' consent.
  • Every game is supervised by the Case Game team. A player who ignores instructions may be removed from the game.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I play Watersoft in Douala?

At Case Game, on our field in New Bell, Douala. Games are supervised by our staff and cost 300 FCFA.

Does it hurt?

Very little: the water-soaked gel beads burst on impact. It feels much lighter than paintball or airsoft, and the protective gear provided must be worn for the whole game.

What is the minimum age?

Watersoft is open to players from age 8. Under-18s play with their parents' consent.

Do I need to bring my own equipment?

No. Blasters, beads and protective gear are provided by Case Game. Just wear comfortable clothes.

Can I book for a group?

Yes. For a birthday or a group outing, message us on WhatsApp at +237 6 86 84 44 07 to agree on a time slot.
