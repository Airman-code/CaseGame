Association Case Game

“It's also about growing.” We use games as a tool to help young people aged 10 to 25 succeed at school, gain confidence and get involved in their neighbourhood.

Support the campaign What we do

Our mission

The Association Case Game is a non-profit, non-political and non-religious association, registered in Cameroon on 8 December 2025 (receipt no. 000879/RDA/J06/SAAJP/BAPP). It extends the work of the Case Game venue in New Bell: making entertainment accessible and using it to help young people thrive.

We welcome all young people, without distinction, in a caring and safe setting. Games capture their attention, encourage them to try things out and help each other, and make learning motivating.

What we do

Tutoring

Homework help from primary school to the final year, exercises suited to each level and regular follow-up, with Games Points to reward effort. Discover our tutoring.

Free workshops

Workshops open to young people and residents of Douala to explore African history, geography, the environment, art and citizenship, in a friendly and interactive atmosphere.

Gamer Solidaire et Vert programme

Young people suggest and carry out useful missions for the neighbourhood: helping residents, clean-ups, recycling. Every approved mission is rewarded with Games Points. Join the programme (form in French).

Social action

Days with the children of the “L'Arche de Mummy” orphanage and actions to support girls.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Association Case Game officially registered?

Yes. The Association Case Game is a non-profit association registered under Law no. 90/053 of 19 December 1990, with receipt no. 000879/RDA/J06/SAAJP/BAPP dated 8 December 2025.

What are donations used for?

The 2026 campaign funds tutoring for 50 children from the “L'Arche de Mummy” orphanage in Douala: personalised exercise books, follow-up, one outing a month and breakfasts. €15 (10,000 FCFA) supports one child for a month.

How can I donate from abroad?

Donations go through Case Game's GoFundMe fundraiser, which accepts bank cards from anywhere in the world. Payment is secure and covered by the GoFundMe Giving Guarantee.

How can I volunteer?

Write to contact.casegame@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp at +237 6 86 84 44 07, telling us what you would like to contribute: homework help, workshops, events or communication.
